Key Caribou Populations in Quebec.

Quebec is home to some pretty unique ecosystems that are extremly vulnerable to disturbances. These habitats are home to a key species in Quebec, Caribou. Caribou are an vulnerable and threatned species in Quebec, what was one a very large and diverse species is now on a quick decline. One of the main reasons to this decline is the loss of vital habitat, largely caused by human activities. Larger protections and actions are needed to protect the Caribous and that starts with knowing what the problem actually is.

This map is for folks in wildlife management.

Key information about this page

Each population group is given a stability level this refers to how vulnerable the habitat is to disturbances in regards to its current condition. It goes on a scale where he vulnerability level is rated on 5. With S1 being the most vulnerable and S5 being the least vulnerable.

The disturbances are also broken down into 7 main categories. There is Cut blocks, which referes to a common practice in forestry where a section of forest is cut down to harvest the trees. There is settlement, which is cities and larget communities. There is Mines, oil and gas which is extraction sites. Then Agriculture and Farming refer to differnt farming practices and land sites.
