Key Caribou Populations in Quebec.

Quebec is home to some pretty unique ecosystems, within these ecosystems are wildlife species that only thrive in these environments. One being Caribou, quebec was once home to a very large and diverse ppopulation. Over the last decade, Caribou have become increasingly vulnerable. One of the main threats to this vital species is the loss of their habitat due to human activities. As well as an increasing number of trafic and roads going through their fragile habitats. The goal of this map is to be able to see this crutial habitats alongside the Quebec road networdk to be able to pinpoint areas of concern to furthr investigate.

This map is for folks in wildlife management.

How to use this map: You can navigate through different Caribou populations and groups, as well as click on them to various key information such as their vulnerability level and threat level. The vulnerability level is given on a scale of S1 -S5 with S1 being the most vulnerable and S5 being the least vulnerable.
